Two small tools for your home server

Both run on your own server, need no account and send nothing to a cloud. Take the one you need — or both.

  • Instant sharing for your home network — like AirDrop, but for every device and every system.

    • Drop in text, files or a screenshot — it is on every other device at once.
    • All in the browser: no app to install, no login, no cloud.
    • A Docker container on your own server, in 17 languages.

    Visit the sitedropnook.dropnook.app GitHub

    AGPL-3.0 · free, open source, made for Docker and Unraid

  • A small office for your Unraid server — a team of characters, each looking after one part of it.

    • Snapshots and nightly backups, with a restore drill that proves they come back.
    • Logs, a night watch on security, and tidying up what nobody uses any more.
    • A plugin for Unraid 7, behind Unraid’s login — no cloud, no account.
